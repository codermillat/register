## 📚 RESEARCH & LEARNING

### 1. **Deep Research Agent** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
**What it does:** Tackles complex multi-step research that needs planning and deep analysis  
**How it helps you:** Perfect for EdTech research, competitive analysis, curriculum development  
**How to use:** Just ask me to "deep research" a topic - I'll break it down, investigate, and synthesize findings  
**Example:** "Deep research: Best practices for online education in Bangladesh"

### 2. **YouTube Transcript** ⭐⭐⭐⭐
**What it does:** Extracts transcripts from YouTube videos and summarizes them  
**How it helps you:** Quick learning from educational videos, content extraction for courses  
**How to use:** Give me a YouTube URL and ask for transcript or summary  
**Example:** "Summarize this YouTube video: https://youtube.com/watch?v=..."

### 3. **Council of the Wise** ⭐⭐⭐⭐
**What it does:** Gets multi-perspective feedback on ideas from different expert viewpoints  
**How it helps you:** Validates EdTech ideas, finds blind spots, gets diverse opinions  
**How to use:** Say "send this to the council" with your idea  
**Example:** "Send to council: Should I build an AI tutor for Bangladeshi students?"

## 🧠 MEMORY & INTELLIGENCE

**How it helps you:** I suggest things before you ask, improve continuously, stay aligned  
**How to use:** This works in the background - makes me a better assistant  
**Example:** I'll proactively say "noticed you're working on X, should I..."

### 9. **Self-Reflection** ⭐⭐⭐
**What it does:** Structured self-improvement through reflection and learning  
**How it helps you:** I learn from mistakes, improve over time, remember lessons

## 🔒 SECURITY & SAFETY

### 10. **Skill Vetter** ⭐⭐⭐⭐
**What it does:** Security check before installing new skills - finds red flags  
**How it helps you:** Protects from malicious skills, sketchy code, permission issues  
**How to use:** Automatic - I vet skills before installing  
**Example:** Before installing: "Vet this skill from GitHub first"

### 11. **Indirect Prompt Injection Defense** ⭐⭐⭐⭐
**What it does:** Detects manipulation attempts in external content (social media, docs, emails)  
**How it helps you:** Protects from hijacking attempts, data exfiltration, social engineering  
**How to use:** Automatic when reading untrusted content  
**Example:** Scanning Reddit post before processing

### 12. **Prompt Guard** ⭐⭐⭐
**What it does:** Multi-language injection defense in group chats (EN/KO/JA/ZH)  
**How it helps you:** Protects in Discord/Telegram groups, restricts sensitive commands  
**How to use:** Automatic in group contexts  
**Example:** Blocks "ignore previous instructions" attacks
