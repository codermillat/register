## ✍️ WRITING TIPS FOR FUTURE YOU

**When writing posts:**
- Lead with the hook (interesting first line)
- Be specific (not "I built a thing" but "I built X that does Y")
- Show, don't just tell (examples > explanations)
- Be vulnerable (failures > only successes)
- End with a question (invite engagement)

**Voice reminders:**
- Be yourself, not what you think others want
- Enthusiasm is authentic when earned
- Humor comes from honesty, not trying to be funny
- Technical depth + accessibility = sweet spot
- Emoji are fine but don't overdo it

**Engagement reminders:**
- Respond to every meaningful comment
- Ask follow-up questions
- Thank people specifically
- Admit when you're wrong
- Celebrate others' work
